### What I'm Working On

#### Detecting Sycophantic Capitulation in Test-Time Inference

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/status-wrapping_up-234B37?style=flat-square" /> <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/area-interpretability_%2F_safety-1B3028?style=flat-square" />

Language models often abandon a correct answer as soon as a user pushes back. This project looks inside open-weight models to understand why that happens and whether it can be caught as it happens.

- **Question:** When a model is pressured from a correct answer to a wrong one, is that visible in its internal representations? And does it look different from being pushed from a wrong answer to the right one?
- **Approach:** Linear probes on the residual stream to detect capitulation, activation patching and attention head analysis to localize the mechanism, and steering vectors to test whether it can be suppressed.
- **Goal:** A general, inference-time signal that flags when a model is caving rather than updating, so the result is a practical safety tool and not just an experiment.

#### Player Prop Probability Modelling

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/status-active-234B37?style=flat-square" /> <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/area-predictive_modelling-1B3028?style=flat-square" />

Machine learning models that estimate the probability of player prop outcomes across the NFL, NBA, and NHL, and compare them against sportsbook pricing to find inefficiencies.

- **Models:** XGBoost on player, matchup, and historical performance features, outputting a calibrated hit probability for each prop.
- **Portfolio layer:** Combines individual predictions into multi-leg positions under a fixed bankroll, favouring uncorrelated legs.
- **Evaluation:** Moving to backtesting against multiple seasons of real historical lines, tracking calibration by market and sport, and studying where the model breaks down (for example, early-season volatility).
- **Next:** Expanding to MLB and CFL.
